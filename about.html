The short version

I trained as a teacher. I found my craft in keeping things in order.

My degree from Obafemi Awolowo University is in Education and Chemistry — a background that taught me precision, patience, and how to explain complicated things simply. Those instincts turned out to be exactly what remote teams needed.

Over the last five years I've supported founders and companies across Nigeria and the UK — managing inboxes and calendars, running end-to-end recruitment support, and keeping social channels and records in good order. In 2024 I sharpened those skills through ALX Academy's virtual assistant training.

The through-line has never changed: I take the scattered, time-consuming parts of a busy role and turn them into something quiet and dependable.
